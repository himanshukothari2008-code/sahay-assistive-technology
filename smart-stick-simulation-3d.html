The blue module on the cane is the front ultrasonic sensor: it finds stones and other obstacles up to 1.5 m away. The purple module looks down at the ground ahead: a reading much longer than normal means the ground has dropped away, so it warns of a pothole with a different double beep. Click once inside the simulation to allow sound.
